About the school

A school where every girl is known, stretched and sent out ready.

Vanessa Grant Girls' School sits in Rongai, Nakuru — a boarding school built on the conviction that girls thrive when high academic expectation is matched by genuine pastoral care. Rigorous teaching, a lively Christian community and one of the busiest co-curricular programmes in Kenya sit side by side here.

Our girls leave well equipped to become exceptional leaders in whatever field they choose to enter.

Read our vision & mission

Our vision

A happy, disciplined school that helps girls achieve very high standards in all they do.

We provide an environment and resources for girls and all staff to achieve excellence in each area of school life. Rigorous academic training is provided, and each girl is expected to avail herself of every opportunity for learning. Christian values and the promotion of the Christian faith in the Anglican tradition permeate all aspects of the school.

Our mission

To provide the very best in boarding education for girls in Kenya.

A warm and happy family environment, day and night, through term. High standards of teaching across every subject — with a special focus on Science and Information Technology. A pastoral care system that takes an active interest in every person in the school community. And as many bursaries as possible for bright girls from poor backgrounds, so that opportunity is shared.

Our campus

A serene setting in Rongai, built for the work of learning.

Quiet grounds, well-kept by a grounds team the school named its outstanding department of 2024. Laboratories, an art room, a computer laboratory and the Bethel Chapel at the heart of it. The calm is not incidental — it is what makes long study, real practicals and honest conversation possible.

See our facilities

Beyond the classroom

Science and IT get a special focus here — and it shows.

The Robotics Club meets every Tuesday, 4:00–5:30pm, with a professional trainer covering programming and mechanics from the ground up — beginners welcome. Alongside it, girls sit the International Computer Driving Licence, leaving school with a certification employers and universities recognise.

One school, three horizons

Where our girls learn, and how far it takes them.

Curiosity, early and often

Our vibrant junior students learn in small, lively classes as the school transitions to the Competency-Based Curriculum. Confidence is built early — junior girls have travelled to Rwanda to compete in the East African regional debate circle.

Rigour, with room to specialise

Six departments carry the senior programme — Sciences, Mathematics, Languages, Humanities, Applied & Technicals, and Co-curricular Activities — with laboratory and computer work threaded through. KCSE results are published each year for families to read.

Service, region, and a lasting network

Girls learn to be of service to others both within and outside the school — through St John Ambulance, Red Cross and the environmental club — and they carry Vanessa Grant into the wider Rongai community and beyond. Our alumni association gathers on campus for reunions.
